Room for your data.

Add storage nodes, inspect their blocks, and remove them through HDFS decommissioning.

HDFS storage Loading…

Three available node slots · live metrics from NameNode

Add: starts a DataNode with its own volume. New writes can use it; existing blocks move when you rebalance.

Remove safely: asks HDFS to decommission the node, waits for the required replicas to exist elsewhere, then stops the container. Its volume is kept. The primary DataNode stays configured.

First setup: adding a node may briefly restart NameNode to enable decommissioning. Its existing data volume is preserved.

All nodes run on this machine. They share its physical disk capacity. Replication remains 1, and sorting still uses LocalJobRunner with one reducer. This page expands the HDFS demo topology; it does not add physical machines or distributed compute.
